# Luastra community code of conduct

## Our standard

Luastra is committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically curious
community. Participation must remain welcoming regardless of experience,
background, identity, disability, nationality, appearance, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving clear, actionable feedback and assuming good faith while boundaries
  are respected;
- making space for questions and different levels of experience;
- acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and preserving contrary evidence;
- respecting privacy, attribution, licenses, and responsible disclosure.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, doxxing, publishing
private correspondence without permission, or pressuring anyone to disclose
credentials or personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies in Luastra repositories, issue trackers, discussions,
reviews, project events, and public spaces where someone represents Luastra.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue when that would expose the
affected person. Use `support@luastra.dev` as the
private conduct-report route and follow the handling boundary in
[`SUPPORT.md`](./SUPPORT.md). Security vulnerabilities follow
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently remove a participant. Enforcement will consider
context, impact, safety, prior behavior, and whether repair is possible.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a review is prohibited.

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible, but
cannot promise confidentiality where law or immediate safety requires action.

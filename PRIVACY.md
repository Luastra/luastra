# Luastra website and project privacy notice

Effective date: 2026-08-31

This notice describes the limited personal-data handling associated with the
official `luastra.dev` documentation website and direct contact with the
Luastra project. The project is maintained by Viacheslav Beliaev.

It does not describe applications built by third parties with Luastra. Those
applications and their operators have separate data practices and
responsibilities.

## Website

The Luastra project does not intentionally operate advertising, behavioural
analytics, user accounts, or a contact form on `luastra.dev`. A local
presentation preference, such as the selected theme, may be stored on the
visitor's device when that feature is available. The project does not use that
local value to identify the visitor.

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical
request information under the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Repository links and other external destinations are governed by their
respective operators.

## Direct contact

When someone emails a Luastra contact address, the project receives the sender
address, message, attachments, and any other information the sender chooses to
provide. That information is used to answer the enquiry, provide requested
support, coordinate a security report, maintain necessary project records, or
meet applicable legal obligations.

Messages are retained only while reasonably needed for those purposes,
security history, dispute handling, or applicable legal requirements. Relevant
service providers, such as the email and hosting providers, process information
needed to deliver their services. Information is not sold. It may be disclosed
when required by applicable law or necessary to protect the project and its
users.

Do not send credentials, access tokens, unnecessary personal data, or unrelated
private source. Suspected vulnerabilities should follow
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

## Privacy enquiries

Questions about this notice or requests concerning personal information sent
directly to the project may be addressed to
`privacy@luastra.dev`. The project may need enough
information to verify and locate the relevant correspondence before acting on a
request.

Material changes to this notice will be recorded in this repository with a new
effective date. Jurisdiction-specific requirements for future accounts,
analytics, hosted services, payments, or other data-processing features require
a separate review before those features are introduced.
